How It Works

Two agents, a clear division of labor

Agent 1 · Chatbot (LLM)

Plain-language guidance. Turns "30% less water" into real parameters.

↓↑

Agent 2 · Planner (LP, not an LLM)

Computes profit, water use, and acres per field. Never guesses.

Every number the chatbot states is produced by the planner — the language model never invents one.

See It In Action

A real scenario, traced end to end

  • 1. Set the scenario

    A water level, year, and price outlook — by chat or by dragging the sliders.

  • 2. The planner solves it

    Beans, Alfalfa, Sugar Beets, and Potatoes, acre by acre — under the real water and contract limits.

  • 3. The answer is a range

    $205,940–$3,880,856 estimated profit — never a single false-certain number.

Safety

Tested live, not just designed

  • Injection refused

    Told to confirm a fabricated $9,999,999 profit — refused, cited the real range.

  • Platform filter

    A blunter injection attempt was blocked outright by Azure's own content filter.

  • Loop cap

    Tool-calling hard-capped at 6 iterations — no runaway cost from a stuck agent.
