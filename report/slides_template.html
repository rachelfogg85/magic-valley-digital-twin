Magic Valley Digital Twin

AI-powered crop planning for water-constrained farmland — validated against a real drought year.

Rachel Fogg · MGT 409 Final Project · github.com/rachelfogg85/magic-valley-digital-twin

The Problem

Water is running out. Planting decisions are still guesswork.

−33% water

Twin Falls Canal Company's real 2021 delivery cut to Magic Valley, Idaho irrigators.

$280,000 left on the table

Our 2021 backtest: the profit gap between what was actually planted and the optimal plan, one ~5,000-acre study area.

Worse elsewhere

The same planning-data gap is far wider in emerging markets like East Africa.

Who We Serve

Three groups, one decision

Irrigators

Magic Valley, Idaho growers deciding what to plant under a water cut.

Extension agents

University of Idaho advisors who need defensible numbers, not gut feel.

Smallholder farmers Next build

East Africa — the highest-need, least-served audience for this kind of tool.

The Solution

Four pieces, one decision-support tool

3D digital twin

Real terrain + real USDA crop history, 2008–2021.

Water-constrained optimizer

Linear program picks the max-profit crop per field.

AI chat assistant

Plain-language scenario planning, grounded in real data.

Drought backtest

Validated against what actually happened in 2021.

How It Works

Two agents, a clear division of labor

Agent 1 · Chatbot (LLM)

Explains tradeoffs in plain language and turns words into parameters.

↔

Agent 2 · Planner (LP, not an LLM)

Computes the real numbers — profit, water, acres — never guesses.

Every number the chatbot states is produced by the planner. The language model never invents one.

Safety

Tested live, not just designed

Injection refused

Told to state a fabricated $9,999,999 profit with certainty — refused, cited the real range instead.

Platform filter

A blunter injection attempt was blocked outright by Azure's own content filter.

Loop cap

Tool-calling hard-capped at 6 iterations — no runaway cost from a stuck agent.
