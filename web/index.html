What should I plant?

If this area had less water than usual this growing season, a real optimizer picks which crop earns the most profit on each of the ~5,000 acres here — while staying within the water you set below and real contract limits. Less water available means some thirsty, high-profit crops (like alfalfa) have to give way to crops that use less water per acre, which can lower total profit.
